⚖️ ABOUT THE COURTROOM

A place where you can speak freely.

Everyone has feelings, struggles, worries, and things they sometimes can't tell anyone.

This website is a place where you can vent, share your emotions, talk about your problems, ask for advice, or simply let everything out.

There is no judgment here. You don't have to hide your feelings, pretend to be okay, or be afraid of being misunderstood.

Your anonymity matters. You can choose a username unrelated to your real identity. You don't have to share your real name or personal details. The developer does not know who you are in real life just from your messages.

You deserve to be heard, understood, and treated with kindness.

You are welcome here, exactly as you are. ❤️

A privacy reminder: your email and account information are processed by the authentication service and may be accessible to the administrator. Avoid sharing identifying or sensitive details you don't want disclosed.
